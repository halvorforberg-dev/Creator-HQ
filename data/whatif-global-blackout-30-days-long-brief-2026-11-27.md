## Write the answer FIRST
**One-sentence WOW answer:** **Even when electricity comes back after thirty days, clean water, safe medicine, food deliveries and functioning institutions cannot be restored by flipping one switch.** Last frame shows the same New York family at a dry kitchen tap while the skyline has power; neighbors arrive with safe drinking water.

**Physical assumptions:** Independent public transmission/distribution grids worldwide stop supplying power simultaneously for 30 days (fictional assumption; they are not one connected global grid). **Electricity itself does NOT vanish:** charged batteries, off-grid solar and working generators initially function; telecommunications, water, hospital resilience and fuel vary by location, stored capacity and contingency planning. Timing is illustrative NOT a globally applicable countdown. Not everyone dies; no specific casualty numbers. The crisis is systemic dependence, not a magic electromagnetic pulse that destroys all electronics.

## Scientific editorial check
- Power supply failures interrupt multiple independent lifelines; clean water/wastewater and health services often degrade on varying timelines; backup systems have capacity limits. Government references: https://www.nga.org/publications/public-communications-playbook-for-energy-emergencies/ , https://www.nga.org/publications/prioritizing-resilience-best-practices-on-energy-resilience-for-healthcare-facilities/
- FEMA documents water, fuel, communication, transport and logistical dependencies: https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_rev_hurricane_ian_ra_2_0.pdf , https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_restoring-flow-operational-strategies-for-em_012025.pdf .
- Distinguish grid blackout from mysterious disappearance of electricity; cell towers and hospitals can have temporary backups, water tanks create delay, off-grid facilities keep running. The idea of **exactly 30 days** is an editorial hypothetical, not a threat forecast.
- Do not claim global death toll, a precise hour every tower/water system fails, total production cessation, instant fuel depletion, or that all internet infrastructure crashes simultaneously.

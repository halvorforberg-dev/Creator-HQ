## English narration (draft, 74 words; natural pace; record actual voice timing)
What if the sky showed three suns? On some days, it almost does. Those bright twins beside the Sun are called sun dogs. High clouds carry flat, six-sided ice crystals. As sunlight passes through, the crystals bend it like tiny prisms. Bright patches appear to the left and right, sometimes with faint rainbow colors. But look closer: there’s still only one real Sun. The other two are sunlight taking a different path through ice.

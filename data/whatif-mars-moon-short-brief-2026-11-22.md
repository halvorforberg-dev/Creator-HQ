## Physical and factual gates

- **Fixed hypothetical:** the Moon is removed, Mars is magically placed in a sustained comparable orbit at the Moon's current average Earth-centre distance. The replacement mechanism is fiction; avoid claiming it is feasible or that Mars naturally travels there.
- NASA planetary fact sheet lists masses: **Moon 0.07346 × 10²⁴ kg**, **Mars 0.64169 × 10²⁴ kg** (ratio ~8.74); diameters **Moon 3,475 km**, **Mars 6,792 km** (ratio ~1.95).
- NOAA states tide-generating forcing scales as mass divided by distance cubed. Thus, at same distance, **~8.7× lunar tidal forcing**; the **actual local tidal range**, storm surge, inland flooding and orbital evolution are not quantified by this relationship alone.
- Do not say oceans rise instantaneously, every coast is destroyed, all tides rise 9×, or that Mars is destined to collide with Earth. Depict coastal impact as potential/variable, not guaranteed identical worldwide.
- Use labels **“~9× TIDE-RAISING FORCE”** not **“9× HIGHER WAVES.”**

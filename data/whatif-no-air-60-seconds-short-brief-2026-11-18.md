## Five truthful hook options (0–2 seconds)
1. **Selected:** “What if Earth's air vanished for one minute?” — immediate atmosphere-off image and readable `NO AIR · 60s` on a phone.
2. “What if the whole sky lost its air?”
3. “What if the atmosphere vanished — right now?”
4. “What if airplanes suddenly had no air?”
5. “What if the planet became a vacuum for sixty seconds?”

**Viewer curiosity:** Air is gone, yet the city and aircraft are still there. What would fail first, and would returning air undo it?

**Visible payoff:** Atmosphere returns on the exact same shot of a falling airliner; a pilot recovers control, but it is still dangerously low, while a pressurized rescue station helps a person. Restoring gas does NOT rewind the minute of lost lift and low pressure. Avoid a guaranteed crash, death count, or magical instant global recovery.

## Facts, limitations and truthfulness
- This is a **physically impossible instantaneous global removal and return** stipulated as a thought experiment; do not imply a natural trigger or predict exact worldwide casualty totals.
- Without atmospheric scattering the clear daytime sky appears dark, while the direct Sun still shines; do not portray instant global nighttime or total solar darkness. NASA: https://www.nasa.gov/general/what-is-earths-atmosphere/
- Without surrounding gas no aerodynamic wing lift, as NASA states. This does not prove every aircraft crashes in exactly one minute: https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/what-is-lift/
- In rapid exposure to vacuum, loss of consciousness may occur within seconds. NASA documents an accidental decompression event where the subject remained conscious ~14 seconds; no exploding human bodies or universal timing claims: https://imagine.gsfc.nasa.gov/ask_astro/space_travel.html
- Liquid water at ordinary Earth temperatures can boil when surrounding pressure collapses; render **surface** boiling only, no claim the oceans completely evaporate in a minute.
- The atmospheric return is a story assumption. Subsequent flight recovery, injury severity and global shock depend on unknown physical conditions: depict as conditional risk, not mathematically known outcome.
- NASA atmosphere protects against UV; 60 seconds is NOT a supported basis for global frozen Earth or mass extinction from radiation alone. https://www.nasa.gov/general/what-is-earths-atmosphere/

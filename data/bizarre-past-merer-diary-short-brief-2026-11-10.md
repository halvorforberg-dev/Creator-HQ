## Historical evidence and claims boundary
1. **Excavator's scholarly edition:** Pierre Tallet (2017), *Les papyrus de la mer Rouge I. Le Journal de Merer* (P. Jarf A et B), Institut français d'archéologie orientale: https://www.orient-mediterranee.com/les-papyrus-de-la-mer-rouge-i-le-journal-de-merer-p-jarf-a-et-b/ .
2. **Egyptian Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities** confirms the Great Pyramid was originally covered with high-quality Turah/Tura limestone, brought by boat: https://egymonuments.gov.eg/en/monuments/the-great-pyramid/ .
3. **National Geographic** summarizes the archaeological discovery at Wadi al-Jarf in 2013 and Merer's daily ledger of limestone deliveries from Tura to Giza: https://www.nationalgeographic.com/premium/article/red-sea-scrolls-pyramids-egypt .
4. **Research article:** Tallet & Marouard (2014), *The Harbor of Khufu on the Red Sea Coast at Wadi al-Jarf, Egypt*, DOI https://doi.org/10.5615/neareastarch.77.1.0004 .

**DO NOT assert:** this diary is a construction blueprint, identifies how the entire pyramid was built/raised, documents the transport of every stone, proves alien/unknown technology, or that images of the text are exact originals. "Diary" and "shipping record" are accurate summaries. Script must distinguish casing stones from inner masonry, and the Red Sea findspot from the Nile/stone journey.

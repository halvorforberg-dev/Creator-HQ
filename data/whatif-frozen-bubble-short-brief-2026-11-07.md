## Source-verified scientific claim
- **Primary peer-reviewed:** Ahmadi et al., *Nature Communications* 10, 2531 (2019), “How soap bubbles freeze”: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-10021-6 . In sufficiently cold, near-uniform ambient conditions, ice crystals can detach from a freezing front and swirl around the *thin bubble film* due to thermal surface-tension-driven Marangoni flow; then interlock as the bubble freezes. In warmer surrounding air over a cold base, freezing may instead halt partway. Don't promise the effect happens with every bubble or temperature. No snow physically falling **inside** the bubble.

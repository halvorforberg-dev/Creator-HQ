## Research and caveat
Primary: https://scrollprize.org/firstscroll (Vesuvius Challenge, 25 June 2026).
Additional: https://www.reuters.com/science/complete-text-carbonised-herculaneum-scroll-unlocked-first-time-2026-06-25/
Herculaneum scroll PHerc. 1667 was carbonized during Vesuvius eruption in AD 79. Using X-ray scans, virtual unwrapping and machine learning, researchers read the surviving inner core (around 1.4 m, c. 22 columns) in June 2026. Outer layers had been lost during earlier physical-opening attempts, so *not* the entire original scroll survived or was reconstructed. The surviving Greek philosophical text has a Stoic context, probably 2nd-century BCE; not a Bible discovery, map or proven treasure. Some transcription remains fragmentary.
